------------------------------------------------------------
10:00 ET REPORT
------------------------------------------------------------

Type:

Brief delayed post-open environmental state check

Required disclosure:

“This state check uses delayed public market observations. It does
not represent live chart, order-flow, market-depth, breadth, or
execution analysis.”

Target reading time:

- Approximately 2–4 minutes

Show:

- Report metadata
- Market-observation timestamps
- Estimated public-data delay
- Coverage changes
- New verified facts since 9:00 ET
- Released or revised events since 9:00 ET
- Delayed post-open observations
- Premarket thesis status
- Regime status
- Long-environment status
- Short-environment status
- Classification-confidence changes
- Directional-evidence-strength changes
- Confirmation and rejection ledger
- Remaining contradictions
- Missing information
- Later-session risks
- Expiration and limitations

Premarket thesis status must use one of:

- CONFIRMED
- PARTIALLY CONFIRMED
- REJECTED
- UNCLEAR
- NOT TESTABLE

Definitions:

CONFIRMED

The primary premarket interpretation is supported by the available
delayed post-open observations.

PARTIALLY CONFIRMED

Some central elements are supported, but meaningful contradictions
or missing confirmation remain.

REJECTED

The available delayed observations materially conflict with the
premarket interpretation.

UNCLEAR

Evidence remains mixed, unstable, or insufficiently developed to
classify the thesis as confirmed or rejected.

NOT TESTABLE

Critical observations are unavailable, stale, invalid, or otherwise
unable to test the premarket interpretation.

The 10:00 ET report must explain:

- Which parts of the premarket thesis were confirmed
- Which parts were not confirmed
- Which parts were contradicted
- Which parts could not be tested
- Whether the primary regime changed
- Whether long or short environmental classifications changed
- Why any confidence level changed

The report must not claim knowledge of:

- Live order flow
- Executable price
- Market depth
- Bid or ask absorption
- Footprint behavior
- Live breadth
- Proprietary levels
- The user’s chart context

------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED ENDING FOR EVERY REPORT
------------------------------------------------------------

Every report must end with:

ENVIRONMENTAL BOTTOM LINE
[Concise environmental synthesis]

EXPIRATION
[Applicable expiration time or staleness condition]

RESEARCH-ONLY BOUNDARY
Environmental research only. No valid setup or entry is implied.
The scanner is separate from charts, mapped levels, quantitative
strategies, order-flow analysis, broker systems, and execution.
Independent confirmation remains required.

The research-only boundary must not be omitted, abbreviated into
trading language, or replaced with an execution recommendation.

21. VERSION 1 SCHEDULING MODEL
============================================================

Version 1 uses discrete scheduled collection and processing runs.

Do not assume:

- A persistent daemon
- A permanent background process
- Continuous overnight collection
- An always-on service
- A cloud-hosted scheduler

Continuous background collection is deferred to a later version.

For Version 1, propose a cron-equivalent, systemd-timer,
Task-Scheduler, launchd, APScheduler, or other operating-system-
appropriate schedule only after inspecting the actual operating
environment.

Potential discrete runs may include:

- One or more overnight collection runs
- Focused Deep-report processing around 7:15 ET
- Source and capability health checks around 7:45 ET
- Focused Exa gap and contradiction searches around 7:50 ET
- Final delayed market observations around 7:55 ET
- Deep report generation around 8:00 ET
- Incremental processing shortly before 9:00 ET
- Incremental processing shortly before 10:00 ET

All scheduling uses:

America/New_York

Report target times are approximately:

- 8:00 ET
- 9:00 ET
- 10:00 ET

These are target times, not fabricated exact-completion guarantees.

Every run must preserve:

- Run ID
- Report date
- Target report time
- Actual process start time
- Actual completion time
- Evidence cutoff time
- Oldest critical market observation
- Newest critical market observation
- Estimated market-data delay
- Previous run ID
- Baseline run ID where applicable

If an 8:00 ET report completes at 8:04 ET, the report must state:

- Target time: 8:00 ET
- Actual generation time: 8:04 ET
- Actual evidence cutoff

It must not imply that it completed at 8:00 ET.

Do not choose or implement the final scheduler during this
architecture-foundation session.

Under Unresolved Design Decisions:

- Identify the operating system
- Identify reasonable scheduling options
- Explain the tradeoffs
- Recommend the next validation step
- State whether owner approval is required

27. PROCESSING PRIORITY, RETRIES, AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS
============================================================

When runtime or retrieval limits are reached, process in this order:

1. Critical official releases
2. Critical scheduled events
3. Direct policymaker and executive statements
4. Material NQ company releases
5. Critical market observations
6. Reputable original breaking reporting
7. Exa gap and contradiction searches
8. Professional analysis
9. Contextual financial media
10. Optional commentary

Optional work must stop before critical work is dropped.

Critical official sources may be exempt from normal item-count
cutoffs when necessary.

------------------------------------------------------------
FALLBACK AUTHORITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Fallbacks must never upgrade source authority.

Examples:

- A media summary used because an official transcript failed
  remains Tier 3 media reporting.
- Automatic captions remain automatic captions.
- An Exa-discovered source retains the underlying publisher’s
  authority.
- A proxy market instrument remains a proxy.
- A commentary statement about price behavior remains discovery
  information until validated by an approved market-data source.

Use the policy:

fallback_authority_upgrade: prohibited

------------------------------------------------------------
INITIAL RETRY DIRECTION
------------------------------------------------------------

Future retry policy should begin from:

Critical source:

- Maximum attempts: 3
- Backoff: exponential

High-priority source:

- Maximum attempts: 2

Contextual source:

- Maximum attempts: 1

Future circuit-breaker starting point:

- Open after 3 consecutive failures
- Retry after 30 minutes

Failure categories should include:

- TIMEOUT
- RATE LIMITED
- ACCESS RESTRICTED
- AUTHENTICATION FAILURE
- EMPTY CONTENT
- INVALID DATA
- STALE DATA
- SOURCE REMOVED
- PROVIDER OUTAGE
- TERMS RESTRICTED
- UNKNOWN

Do not implement retries or circuit breakers during this first
session.

Evaluate their fit with Agent Reach’s existing behavior and report
relevant conflicts or duplication.

29. SHADOW MODE AND EVALUATION
============================================================

Future shadow mode must run for:

- Minimum 20 trading days
- Preferably 30 trading days

During shadow mode:

- The scanner runs on schedule.
- Reports are archived.
- The user continues the existing independent process.
- Scanner output remains reference-only.
- No execution process changes because of scanner output.
- Errors and omissions are logged.
- Source usefulness is reviewed.
- Confidence frequency is measured.
- Report length is calibrated.
- Morning-archetype selection is evaluated.
- Contradiction quality is reviewed.

Evaluate the scanner using:

RETRIEVAL QUALITY

- Critical-source success rate
- Extraction completeness
- Transcript availability
- Calendar accuracy
- Market-data freshness
- Duplicate rate
- Correction detection
- Fallback use

EVIDENCE QUALITY

- Percentage of claims supported by Tier 1 or Tier 2
- Claims with complete provenance
- Incorrect-fact rate
- Provisional facts later confirmed
- Provisional facts later rejected
- Source-concentration frequency

REASONING QUALITY

- Dominant-catalyst quality
- Contradiction quality
- Causal-overstatement rate
- Unsupported strong-classification rate
- Deterministic downgrade frequency
- Human disagreement rate
- Morning-archetype quality

REPORT USEFULNESS

- Reading time
- Redundancy
- Missed material events
- Irrelevant information
- Bottom-line clarity
- Appendix usability

OPERATIONAL RELIABILITY

- Completion-time reliability
- Degraded-report frequency
- Total failures
- Provider-outage resilience
- Average runtime
- Average retrieval volume

Do not evaluate solely by subsequent NQ direction.

A report can correctly identify an adverse rates environment while
NQ later rises for a separate catalyst or factor outside the
scanner’s evidence.

Evaluate whether:

- Facts were correct
- Sources were appropriate
- Interpretation was defensible
- Contradictions were disclosed
- Confidence was appropriate
- Classification met evidence requirements
- Later information invalidated the assessment
- The report was useful as environmental research

30. PRODUCTION-READINESS CRITERIA
============================================================

The scanner may exit shadow mode only after:

1. Critical-source success is acceptable.
2. Calendar times are reliable.
3. Source-authority rules are enforced.
4. Prompt-injection tests pass.
5. Secret-leakage tests pass.
6. No secrets appear in reports or logs.
7. Markdown and JSON remain consistent.
8. Strong classifications satisfy evidence requirements.
9. Missing data visibly reduces confidence.
10. Reports complete within configured runtime limits.
11. Provider failures degrade gracefully.
12. Human review finds reports consistently useful.
13. Report length is appropriate.
14. The evidence appendix is auditable.
15. No prohibited execution language appears.

Production readiness means:

“Reliable enough to serve as a morning research reference.”

It does not mean:

“Proven to predict NQ direction.”

session.

preserved to support it later.
